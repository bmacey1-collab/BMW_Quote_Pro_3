# BMW Quote Pro

## Mission

BMW Quote Pro is a professional dealership desking, quoting, and customer presentation application designed specifically for BMW dealerships.

The goal is not simply to calculate payments.

The goal is to help salespeople, managers, and customers understand and compare deal structures accurately while reducing mistakes and improving transparency.

---

# Primary Objectives

BMW Quote Pro should:

- Produce accurate lease calculations.
- Produce accurate retail finance calculations.
- Produce accurate BMW Select calculations.
- Compare multiple deal scenarios.
- Save and retrieve customer quotes.
- Manage dealership programs and incentives.
- Generate professional customer quotes.
- Generate manager worksheets for deal verification.
- Become a complete portfolio management system for dealership salespeople.

---

# Philosophy

Accuracy is more important than appearance.

Business logic is more important than animation.

Every financial calculation should be explainable.

Every important number should be verifiable.

The application should save time while increasing customer confidence.

Client Advisor

Customer

- Compare financing methods

Never reuse formulas between transaction types unless they truly represent identical business logic.
